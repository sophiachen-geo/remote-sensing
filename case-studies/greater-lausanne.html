Each bar is one measurement point, numbered to match the circles on the map. Hover a bar to read the station name, setting and field note. The dotted lines mark CO₂ references: about 420 ppm outdoor background (NOAA), the 600 to 800 ppm indoor air quality target (REHVA) and the classic 1000 ppm ventilation guideline (Pettenkofer). The workplace limit is far higher at 5000 ppm over 8 hours (OSHA). Bars show each point's mean; individual peaks reach 1693 ppm on the M1 metro.
Indoor points (metro rides, the railway station, shops and campus interiors) average several hundred ppm above the outdoor points, which sit near background. The gap is large and statistically significant: CO₂ here is driven by enclosed, occupied, poorly ventilated spaces rather than by outdoor location.
Across outdoor points, NO₂ pollution is highest on the low valley floor and drops steeply with altitude. This is the strongest relationship in the survey. For health context the World Health Organization 2021 guideline is 10 µg/m³ (annual) and the European Union limit is 40 µg/m³, but those are surface concentrations and are not directly comparable to this satellite column, so no threshold line is drawn.
Outdoor points with more vegetation (higher NDVI) have cooler land surface temperatures: greenery cools the surface. For reference, a healthy indoor minimum is 18 °C (World Health Organization) and seated comfort is about 20 to 26 °C (ASHRAE), while outdoor heat stress begins above 26 °C (UTCI); these describe air temperature, whereas the axis shows land surface temperature, which reads warmer in sun.
Pearson correlation for every pair of point level variables, computed on the outdoor points. Blue means the two move in opposite directions, red means they rise together. Cells with a black box and bold number are statistically significant (stars: * p<.05, ** p<.01, *** p<.001); faint grey numbers are not significant. The diagonal is omitted because a variable always correlates perfectly with itself.
All satellite layers sampled over the field measurement period. Sources: Local Climate Zones (WUDAPT), GHSL building height and built area, Landsat 8 land surface temperature, Sentinel 5P NO₂, Sentinel 2 true colour.
